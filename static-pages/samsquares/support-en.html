How do I play?

Each puzzle shows a picture made of squares. Drag squares from the row under it onto the empty grid to build the same picture. Squares with two colors can be turned: tap one in the row or on the grid to rotate it a quarter turn. When the grid matches the picture, the puzzle is solved.

How do I earn three stars?

Every solved puzzle earns a star. A second star comes from finishing within the level's time limit, and a third from solving without extra moves: one drag per square and no square turned all the way around.

What are the mirror levels?

In the Mirror levels you build the picture as it would look in a mirror: the left side becomes the right side. The game explains it before the first mirror puzzle.

Why can't I open some puzzles?

Puzzles open one after another: solve a puzzle to open the next one. Solved puzzles can always be played again, and replaying never lowers the stars you already have.

How do I start a level again?

Open Settings (the gear in a level's puzzle list) and choose Clear level, or Clear all levels to start the whole game again.

How do I change the music, sounds and vibration?

The note button turns all sound on or off. Settings has separate controls for the music volume, the sound effects volume and haptic feedback.

Will I lose my stars?

Stars are saved on the device and kept when the app is updated, including from version 1. They aren't shared between devices. They're included in the device's backups, and they're removed if the app is deleted.
